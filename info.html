  • International shipping available to select countries at checkout

Pearl & Jewelry Care

Pearls are organic and softer than most gemstones — a little care keeps them luminous for decades.

  • Put pearls on last, after perfume, lotion, and hairspray
  • Wipe with a soft cloth after each wear
  • Store flat or hanging, away from other jewelry that could scratch the surface
  • Complimentary cleaning and restringing is available any time — just reach out to book a service
