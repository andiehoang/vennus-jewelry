The Maison

Quiet by design.

Our Story

Vennus began with a single strand of pearls and the belief that fine jewelry didn't need to shout to be noticed.

Vennus was founded on a simple idea: that a pearl, worn well, says more than a room full of diamonds. We work with a small circle of graders and setters to source pearls for their lustre and character first, building each collection slowly, in limited runs, rather than chasing every season's trend.

Every piece that carries the Vennus name is inspected by hand before it leaves our studio — a habit we've kept since our very first order.

Atelier or founder photo

Craftsmanship

Each piece passes through five sets of hands before it reaches yours.

Grading

Every pearl is hand-sorted for lustre, shape, and surface before it's approved for a collection.

Setting

Settings are cast in small batches in 14k gold, gold vermeil, or sterling silver — never plated base metal.

Finishing

Clasps and closures are tested by hand for a full year of daily wear before a design goes into production.

Inspection

A final quality check happens the day your order is packed, not before — so what you receive is exactly what was approved.

Materials or packaging photo

Sustainability

We produce in small, considered runs rather than holding deep inventory — it costs us convenience, but it means very little goes to waste. Packaging is recyclable and printed with soy-based ink, and our gold is recycled wherever the setting allows.

We're not a perfect supply chain yet. We publish what we know about our sourcing and update it as we learn more — reach out any time with questions.
